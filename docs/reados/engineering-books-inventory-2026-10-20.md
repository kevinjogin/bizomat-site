## Gap assessment

The library already covers CAD practice, tolerance fundamentals, workholding, fixtures, quality, automation, product development and project delivery. Another general introduction would duplicate existing coverage. Business and Technology have the largest book counts. The strongest opportunities in this batch are connected engineering casebooks that make the existing introductory material executable: a complete dimensional release decision and a fixture qualification method.

**Fits That Work** adds a continuous signed stack, thermal envelope, geometric allowance, measurement decision, pilot interpretation and revision-controlled release. **Fixtures That Repeat** adds a continuous locating/load/stiffness/access/trial/maintenance qualification case. Each has ten original chapters, new numerical examples and exercises. Neither republishes an existing READOS chapter.

The supplied *Machinery's Handbook*, 29th edition (Industrial Press, 2012), was examined selectively for topic coverage and historical technical background. Its contents and relevant dimensional/measuring sections were reviewed; it was not treated as a current standard or copied. Primary online references are itemised in each new book and the accompanying editorial report.
