A business is not just a sales target. It is a system of customers, obligations, decisions, cash movements, people, information and time. This KEVOS® handbook turns those moving parts into practical disciplines you can learn, practise and review.

**How to use this book.** Read each chapter in order for a complete operating framework, or use a specific chapter when solving a current problem. Complete the exercise at the end of each chapter. Keep the worksheets in a private business record, not in a public AI chat. All numerical examples are hypothetical, Australian-dollar illustrations, generally excluding GST unless stated.

**Editorial note.** This is independently written KEVOS educational content. It contains no event transcripts, speaker identities, customer records, confidential project details or reproduced presentation slides. The methods are general business knowledge, not exclusive intellectual property. This guide is educational information, not individual financial, legal, tax or professional advice.
