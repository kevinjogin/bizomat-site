# Design audit, 15 November 2026

The owner asked: "go through the full site and check if there is any design flaws or inconsistencies and rectify and
deploy it". The example was the homepage at about 1850 px: the button "Explore Engineering Automation" ran past its
card into the next one, the "Customer Relationship Management" heading took three big lines, and each card's
"Formerly" line and sign-in chip sat unevenly above the heading.

Deployed on 15 Nov 2026 (kevinjogin/bizomat-site#75, deploy run 38, version 68dc30f). The layout audit run against
https://bizomat.ai after the deploy (`--base https://bizomat.ai --viewports 1440,390`, both themes, 1714 internal links)
found no layout error and no broken link; its warnings are the ones under "Not changed, and why", clamped Library card
excerpts, and table text a few pixels into its padding in two Library chapters.

## What was checked

- **Pages:** one or more of every page template (`scripts/design-audit/pages.mjs`, 50 pages): the homepage, Systems
  and every system's page and guide (WHS included), Resources, Tools (the index, an open calculator and three tool
  pages), the Library (index, a book, a collection, a publication and their chapters), Guides, the Engine Room,
  Contact, the legal pages, Search, Sign in, the 404 page, Industries, Solutions, Services, How it works, Factory Fix,
  the Assessment and the assistant's pop-out page.
- **Widths and themes:** 1920, 1440, 1280, 1024, 768, 390 and 360 px, light and dark: 700 page loads, in Chromium,
  served by the website's own Node server, so the real headers and Content-Security-Policy apply.
- **Checks** (`scripts/design-audit/checks.mjs`): a page wider than the screen; content outside its card or box; text
  cut off by `overflow: hidden`; text on top of other text; grid or flex items on top of each other; button labels
  that overflow, run past their parent or take three lines; images without a width and height, or stretched; text
  below WCAG AA contrast; controls without a visible focus ring, or with one under 3:1; an H1 count other than one;
  broken internal links and anchors; console errors and CSP violations. SVG text is left to the eye: it sits on the
  drawing's own shapes, which the contrast check cannot see.
- **By eye:** screenshots of the key pages at 1440 and 390 in both themes: spacing, alignment, card heights in a row,
  button and chip styles, heading scale, section spacing, dark surfaces, the header, its menus, the phone menu and the
  footer.
- **BIZOMAT ERP** (`apps/work`, signed in on the local Node server with the example company): the module rail, the
  side menu, cards, tables and buttons of the main modules at 1440 and 390 in both themes.

## What was found and fixed

On the code as it was (2e1b681f), the checks found 67 distinct errors (308 across all widths and themes) on 26 pages.
After the fixes there are none.

| Flaw | Where | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Not changed, and why

- **Long names on three lines.** Tool names ("Ball Bearing Load Ratings from Geometry (...)"), some book and case-study
  titles and some feature headings wrap to three lines in their cards. That is the length of the content, not the
  layout; the cards keep their actions aligned.
- **Illustrations on phones.** Diagrams such as the systems map scale down as pictures, so their labels are very small
  at 390 px. Each carries its caption and the "Illustration" label; redrawing them for phones is a design task of its
  own.
- **Avatar colours in BIZOMAT ERP.** Initials are white on a fixed palette (`apps/work/src/client/lib/format.ts`); most
  colours give under 4.5:1 (sky blue 2.8:1, amber under 2:1). Darker shades of the same hues would pass. It is a colour
  change in the app, not a layout flaw, so it is left for a decision.
- **kevos.com on the Assessment page.** The sample report's footer, the hero drawing and the QR code (a generated
  asset) name kevos.com, the former address. Changing them needs new QR assets and is a content decision.
- **Old Insights category redirects** (`public/_redirects`) send `/insights/category/<subject>/` to `/reados/#<subject>`,
  an anchor that does not exist (the page opens at the top). Tests pin these targets; they are old addresses.
- **The Assessment's example tabs** wrap to three lines in two columns at 390 px. They are readable tiles, not buttons.

## How to run it

```bash
npm run build && npm run build:server
npm run audit:design   # the full audit: every template, seven widths, both themes, screenshots in .design-audit/
npm run audit:layout   # the CI check: the main templates at 1440 and 390 px in both themes, about 30 seconds
```

`audit:layout` runs in CI's website job (`.github/workflows/bizomat-ci.yml`) after the build and fails on any error
above. It first loads a fixture page with one of each flaw (`scripts/design-audit/self-test.mjs`), so a check that
stops working fails instead of passing quietly. Playwright 1.63.0 is a root devDependency (the version `apps/work`
uses); the job installs Chromium's headless shell and keeps the report as an artifact when the check fails. A new page
template, such as a new system's page and guide, belongs in `scripts/design-audit/pages.mjs`.
