## Verify before publishing

- **Rules of thumb attributed to the book** (gross margin ≥ 40%; SG&A under ~30% of gross profit; interest under ~15% of operating income; long-term debt repayable in 3–4 years of earnings; adjusted debt-to-equity under ~0.80; capex under 25–50% of net earnings; net margin ~20%+ strong / under 10% weak; P/E ~40+ as a sell signal). These were checked against the book's text but should be re-read against your copy.
- **Historical statements** in *from-bargains-to-quality* (Buffett studied under and worked for Graham; Graham's disciplines) and *institution-or-people* (Salomon Brothers): summarised from the book; widely documented, but confirm wording.
- **Berkshire Hathaway's retained-earnings policy** (*retained-earnings-the-compounding-engine*): stated generally ("for most of its history paid no dividend"); confirm.
- **Owner earnings** (*why-the-cash-flow-statement-keeps-a-business-honest*): attributed to Buffett's shareholder letters (the 1986 letter is the usual reference); confirm before adding a specific citation.
- **Accounting statements** (*the-assets-the-balance-sheet-cannot-see*): goodwill impairment rather than amortisation, and non-recognition of internally generated brands, reflect current Australian standards (AASB 136 and AASB 138, aligned with IFRS). Have an accountant confirm the wording.
- **Tax note** (*share-buybacks-and-treasury-stock*): mentions that Australian franking credits change the dividend-versus-buyback comparison. Keep it general; do not add specific tax claims without advice.
- The **New Coke** reference (*owning-a-place-in-the-customers-mind*) is described without naming the company or dates; confirm you are comfortable with it.

## Publishing-risk notes

- **Copyright:** the articles summarise and comment on ideas and attribute them; they do not reproduce the book's text, structure chapter by chapter, examples or figures. Keep it that way when editing (no long quotations).
- **Financial advice:** each article is general education about reading financial statements and building businesses, with a disclaimer. Do not add personal recommendations, share tips or forecasts.
- **GoCore positioning:** the articles do not claim GoCore offers financial, investment or accounting services.
