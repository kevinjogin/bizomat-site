## Technical review

- Units are converted explicitly: bar to N/mm², litre flow to displacement, millimetres to metres, centistokes to m²/s, and Celsius to Kelvin where absolute temperature is required.
- Hydraulic forces retain negative values when opposing pressure dominates. Extension and retraction use the relevant piston areas.
- Beam models state their support/load cases and use total force for the uniformly distributed case. The tools establish no allowable load.
- Belt length uses exact tangent-and-arc geometry; pulley pitch conventions and overlap constraints are explicit.
- Pneumatic demand states pressure and temperature reference conditions, and distinguishes mean demand from peak/compressor selection.
- Fit limits use signed diametral clearance. Capability indices distinguish within-process sigma from overall performance and do not infer an acceptance threshold.
- Zero recorded failures never produce an infinite MTBF claim. Availability is described as a summary of the observation window.
- Empty, non-finite, out-of-range, fractional-count and inconsistent geometry inputs produce **CHECK INPUTS** without a numerical primary result. Invalid shared inputs are not silently replaced with example values.
- Fine non-zero values retain significant digits rather than becoming zero in the display. Reports and copied summaries recalculate from the current inputs before export.
