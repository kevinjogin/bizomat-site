# KEVOS brand and homepage update

The approved public names are KEVOS®, WORKOS, READOS and REACHOS. WORKOS replaces the previous tools product name and remains at `/tools/`. REACHOS replaces the previous contact product name and remains at `/contact/`. Existing book, article and service URLs are retained.

## Verified registration

IP Australia's public record for [Australian word trade mark 2469816](https://search.ipaustralia.gov.au/trademarks/search/view/2469816) was read on 15 October 2026. It shows KEVOS as Registered/protected in class 42, registered from 29 July 2024 and entered on the register on 7 March 2025. The website describes the registration for the engineering, design and related services specified in the register. No separate registration is claimed for WORKOS, READOS or REACHOS, and no owner's personal details have been added to the site.

`SITE.tradeMark` is the shared source for registration details. `TradeMarkNotice.astro` provides the notice in Terms and Disclaimer. The footer links to the record. Privacy identifies the site areas and accurately describes browser-side calculations and session storage for the display preference. Existing consumer-rights and professional-certification provisions remain in place.

## Homepage illustrations

`HomeResourceVisual.astro` provides two original SVG/CSS scenes. READOS has a turning book page, book spines and chapter markers. WORKOS has moving input sliders, a calculation dial and comparison bars. Both use the existing orange and neutral design tokens, accessible figure captions and static reduced-motion alternatives. The cards share a two-column layout that stacks on mobile. No animation dependency or new endpoint was introduced.

Homepage section 7 is labelled READOS and WORKOS, with the heading “Read, learn and put ideas to work.” Its local navigation label is Resources. The copy describes both books and practical decision tools.
