## The question

What is the least you need to build to learn whether an idea works?

## Points to develop

- A prototype is a question in physical or digital form. Decide the question first.
- Rough prototypes invite honest feedback; polished ones invite politeness.
- Separate "does it work?" prototypes from "does anyone want it?" prototypes.
