## About this book

Policy advice lands when a decision-maker can act on it: the problem is clear, the evidence is sound, the options are real, the costs are honest and the risks are named. Advice that misses any of these is either ignored or, worse, accepted and then fails in delivery.

This book follows one policy proposal from a minister's request to a Budget decision and beyond. It starts with defining the problem, then moves through evidence and consultation, generating options, impact analysis, costing, new policy proposals and the Budget, writing for Cabinet, advising the minister, planning implementation, and finally evaluation and review. Each chapter ends with an exercise and a worked answer.

The chapters use **the Strategy and Policy Branch of the Northfield Regional Infrastructure Office**, a fictional agency, as a continuous example. Its staff, survey, consultations, options, estimates, costings and decisions are invented for teaching. They are not a real agency, program or decision, a BIZOMAT project or a claim about how any agency, Cabinet or Budget process works. The discount rate, unit rates and dates in the case are the fictional agency's choices.

Much of the policy and Budget process is set by government guidance rather than law: the Cabinet Handbook, the Budget Process Operational Rules, the impact analysis guidance and the evaluation policy. This book describes those processes in general terms and does not quote them. Read the current versions issued by the Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet and the Department of Finance alongside it.

Amounts are in Australian dollars.

## An independent treatment

This is general education, not legal, financial or policy advice, and not an official publication of any agency. Law, guidance and processes change, and every agency has its own procedures for policy, Budget and Cabinet work. Before you act, check the version of each rule in force and your agency's own instructions, and ask your Cabinet liaison, finance or legal area when the consequences matter.
