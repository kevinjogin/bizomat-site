## About this book

A drawn or spun part begins as a flat blank and ends as a cup, a shell, a cover or a cone without a seam. The metal does not stretch much to get there. It flows: the blank's outer ring is pulled inwards and gathered into a wall, its circumference shrinking as its radius falls. Whether that flow succeeds, or the part wrinkles, tears or splits, depends on a few numbers: the blank's size, the ratio of the blank to the punch, the forces on the wall and the flange, the clearance and radii of the tools, and how hard the metal has become from the work already done on it.

This book works through drawing and spinning in the order a designer or manufacturing engineer meets them. It covers what the two processes do to the metal, blank sizes, draw ratios and redraws, drawing force and press capacity, blank holding and wrinkling, die clearance and radii, earing, trim and tolerances, ironing, work hardening and annealing, metal spinning and shear spinning, and the choice between drawing, spinning and other routes.

It suits product designers, manufacturing engineers, toolmakers, press shop supervisors and buyers. It assumes school algebra and a little geometry. Flat-pattern development of seamed sheet metal shapes is covered by *Sheet Metal Patterns by the Numbers*, and general sheet metal part design by *Sheet Metal Parts by the Numbers*; this book is about parts made from one blank without a seam.

## The running case

Every chapter follows one fictional part. This is an illustration. SC-100 is a stainless steel sensor cover: a flat-bottomed cup with a mean diameter of 100 mm, a finished height of 70 mm and a bottom corner radius of 4 mm, drawn from 0.8 mm 304 stainless steel sheet. It protects an instrument on outdoor equipment, it must be seamless so that it seals against its base, and it is wanted in quantities that might be hundreds or tens of thousands a year. The material properties, limits and process figures used are illustrations chosen to show the method; a real part uses the supplier's certified properties, the toolmaker's experience and trials.

The part and its numbers are invented; none describes KEVOS® work or a real product. Prices, where they appear, are illustrations in Australian dollars excluding GST.
