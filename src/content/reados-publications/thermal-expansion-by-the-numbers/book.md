## About this book

Almost everything grows when it warms and shrinks when it cools. The amounts are small, a fraction of a millimetre per metre for every few degrees, and that smallness is the trap. A 60 m hot water pipe grows by more than 70 mm when it warms up. A precision part measured warm can be rejected for an error that is only its temperature. A pipe held rigidly between two anchors can push on them with tens of kilonewtons. A closed tank of water heated without room to expand can burst. And a machine whose shaft and housing grow differently can bind its own bearings. Each of these has a short calculation that predicts it and a design habit that prevents it.

This book works through thermal expansion by numbers. It covers how solids, liquids and gases grow with temperature; measuring parts at the right temperature; the stress and force when growth is prevented; pipe anchors, guides and loops; shrink and expansion fits; expansion of water in closed systems and the vessels that absorb it; gas pressure and temperature; and designing machines for movement. Each chapter works one problem through the running case, shows an error and how it is caught, and ends with an exercise and its worked answer.

## The running case

The book follows **HW-60**, the wash-down hot water system of a fictional food factory, also used in *Heat Transfer by the Numbers*. Its 60 m stainless steel hot water pipe, 60.3 mm in outside diameter with a 2.0 mm wall, carries 85 °C water and is installed at about 15 °C. A closed primary loop of about **400 L** carries heat from a boiler to a plate heat exchanger. The system's pump has a coupling hub shrunk onto its shaft, and an aluminium flowmeter body is machined and inspected in the factory's workshop.

All dimensions, temperatures, pressures, costs and results are invented for teaching. This is an illustration. None of it describes a KEVOS® client, supplier or project. Money is in Australian dollars, excluding GST.

## How to use the book

Chapters 1 and 2 cover growth and its effect on measurement. Chapters 3 to 5 deal with solids that are restrained or fitted together. Chapters 6 and 7 cover liquids and gases in closed systems, and Chapter 8 machines. KEVOS® calculators for several steps include [thermal growth](/tools/thermal-growth/), [thermal stress](/tools/thermal-stress/), [pipe expansion loop](/tools/pipe-expansion-loop/) and [shrink fit design](/tools/shrink-fit-design/).

## Source limits and professional responsibility

The supplied 1925 textbook informed the range of subjects. The methods here are standard physics and mechanics with sources for material data. Allowable stresses, pressure ratings and relief settings in the case are labelled teaching values. Pressure piping, pressure vessels, heated water services and their safety devices are regulated: real designs follow the applicable standards and are checked by competent people, and heating parts for fitting carries burn and fire risks that must be managed.
